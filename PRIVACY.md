# Privacy Policy for Aloud

**Last updated: 13 August 2026**

Aloud is a reading app that narrates your own ebooks aloud on your device. This
policy explains what it does and does not do with information. It is short
because the app does very little with data.

## The short version

Aloud has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics. It does not sell or
share personal information with anyone. Your books, your reading positions and
your bookmarks stay on your device.

## What stays on your device

Everything you add and everything the app records about your reading is stored
locally, in the app's own private storage:

- The EPUB and PDF files you import, and the text extracted from them
- Book covers, chapters and the sentences the narrator reads
- Your reading position, bookmarks, folders and finished markers
- Your settings: theme, font, narrator voice, speed, ambience and pronunciations

None of this is uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## When Aloud uses the internet

The app works offline. It makes network requests in only two situations, both of
which you start yourself:

**Downloading a narrator voice.** If you choose an optional voice from the voice
library, the app downloads that voice's model files from the public repositories
that host them (`huggingface.co`). The request contains no information about you
or what you are reading — only which file is being fetched.

**The pronunciation workshop.** If you browse or download community pronunciation
packs, the app fetches them from their public hosting. If you choose to *submit*
a pack you have made, the pack's contents — the words and pronunciations you
wrote, and any name you put on it — are sent for publication and become publicly
visible. Only submit what you are happy to publish. Nothing is submitted unless
you tap to submit it.

As with any internet request, the servers involved will see your device's IP
address. Aloud does not add any identifier of its own.

## Permissions, and why

- **Internet / network state** — the two cases above, and to avoid starting a
  large download when you are offline.
- **Foreground service, media playback** — so narration keeps playing when the
  screen is off or you switch apps, which is the normal way an audiobook is
  listened to. Without it Android stops the book mid-sentence.
- **Notifications** — to show the playback controls you use to pause, skip and
  stop from outside the app.

Aloud does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone,
call logs, or to files other than the ones you explicitly pick when adding a
book.

## Children

Aloud is not directed at children and collects no personal information from
anyone, including children.
